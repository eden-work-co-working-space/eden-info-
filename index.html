Policy

House rules

Simple rules so everyone can enjoy a peaceful space.

🚭

No smoking or vaping inside

Smoking and vaping are allowed only outside, on the terrace.

🎧

Professional calls only

Personal phone calls are not allowed inside. Professional calls only, and headphones are required.

📞

Personal calls on the terrace

Need to chat with family or friends? Step out to the terrace.

🔔

Self-service

💳

Payment before entry

Please pay at reception before taking your seat.

🤫

Keep it quiet

Speak softly and respect others so nobody is bothered.

What you need to know

  • ⏱️ Choose a 3, 4 or 6 hour package. Your time starts at the start time you choose.
  • 💳 Payment is made at reception before the event starts.
  • ❄️ Available on site: air conditioning, 5G WiFi, tables and chairs, a TV screen and flipchart in the meeting room, coffee and a printer.
  • 🚭 House rules apply: no smoking or vaping inside (the terrace is allowed), and please keep the noise low.
  • 📩 Your request is not final until our team confirms it by phone or email.
  • 📝 We only need your name, phone number, email, and the date and time you want.
